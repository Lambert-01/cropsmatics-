## Why this answers Track 1

Cropmatics uses official NISR agricultural observations to help a planner
identify where a crop-specific productivity shortfall is visible, inspect
associated indicators, compare districts, understand an intervention ranking,
and plan a transparent follow-up scenario. Post-harvest and storage context
extend the decision from production to handling without inventing capacity or
economic returns.

## Evidence boundary

The product demonstrates decision support. It does not claim that a priority
score proves causality, that a forecast exists when validation is insufficient,
or that a planning scenario guarantees yield improvement. This restraint is a
strength for the data/methodology criterion, but field impact remains
unmeasured.
