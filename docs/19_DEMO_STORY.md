# 16. Pitch Story

Opening:
> Producing more is only part of agricultural productivity. Value is also lost when planners cannot identify where productivity gaps are emerging or when harvests arrive where storage and aggregation are not ready.

Demo:
1. Open national dashboard.
2. Select crop/season.
3. Show district productivity gap.
4. Show associated factors with uncertainty.
5. Open intervention priority.
6. Switch to Expo app.
7. Register upcoming harvest.
8. Sync.
9. Compute post-harvest/storage risk.
10. Run storage allocation.
11. Return to planner dashboard.
12. Record intervention.
13. Farmer receives updated recommendation.
14. Show source/model transparency.

Closing:
> Cropmatics connects official statistics to operational action while keeping official estimates and voluntary live signals scientifically separate.
