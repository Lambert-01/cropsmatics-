# 12. Security, Privacy & Ethics

- Collect only necessary data.
- Make GPS/photo optional unless needed.
- Store consent version.
- Never expose exact individual farmer locations publicly.
- Do not call self-selected mobile reports national statistics.
- Use RBAC and audit logs.
- Encrypt transport (HTTPS).
- Keep secrets outside Git.
- Review model errors by geography/crop/farm type.
- Keep intervention decisions human-in-the-loop.
